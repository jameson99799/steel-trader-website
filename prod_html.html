What Is Galvalume Steel Coil?

Galvalume steel coil is a coated steel product produced by applying an aluminum-zinc alloy layer to cold rolled steel through a continuous hot-dip process. In many international markets, this material is also known as Aluzinc steel coil or GL coil. The standard coating composition is approximately 55% aluminum, 43.4% zinc and 1.6% silicon.

This alloy coating combines the barrier protection of aluminum with the sacrificial protection of zinc, giving the steel better long-term atmospheric corrosion resistance in many environments. Because of this, Galvalume steel coil is widely used in roofing systems, wall panels, prefabricated buildings, industrial equipment and other applications where service life and weather resistance are important.

Besides corrosion resistance, Galvalume steel coil also offers a bright metallic appearance, strong heat reflectivity and good processing performance. It can be formed by bending, cutting, corrugating and roll forming, making it a highly practical material for construction manufacturers and project contractors.

For buyers, distributors and steel importers, Galvalume steel coil is a reliable solution when balancing cost, durability, coating quality and long-term project performance.

Galvalume Steel Coil vs Galvanized Steel Coil

Many buyers compare Galvalume Steel Coil with Galvanized Steel Coil (GI Steel Coil) before making a purchasing decision. Although both products are coated steel materials designed to improve corrosion resistance, their coating composition, performance characteristics and recommended applications are different.

Galvalume Steel Coil uses an aluminum-zinc alloy coating, while Galvanized Steel Coil uses a zinc coating. Because of the aluminum content, Galvalume steel usually provides better long-term atmospheric corrosion resistance and stronger heat reflectivity in many outdoor environments. Galvanized steel coil, on the other hand, remains a widely used and cost-effective option for many general construction and fabrication purposes.

Choosing between Aluzinc steel coil and Galvanized steel coil depends on factors such as project environment, expected service life, heat exposure, surface appearance, downstream processing and total project budget.

Frequently Asked Questions About Galvalume Steel Coil

What is the difference between Galvalume Steel Coil and Galvanized Steel Coil?

Galvalume steel coil uses an aluminum-zinc alloy coating, while Galvanized steel coil uses a zinc coating. Galvalume often offers better heat reflectivity and stronger atmospheric corrosion resistance in many outdoor applications.

What does AZ150 mean?

AZ150 refers to the total aluminum-zinc coating mass of 150 g/m² on both sides combined. Higher coating weight usually means better corrosion resistance and longer service life.

Is anti-fingerprint treatment necessary?

Anti-fingerprint treatment is highly recommended for many applications because it improves surface cleanliness, reduces handling marks and adds extra protection during storage and transportation.
